Privacy Policy - Yatzy Score Tracker

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Yatzy Score Tracker ("the App", package name com.jakub.dicegame) is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it is used.

The App itself does not require an account and does not ask you to enter any personal information. The App is a score tracker for a physical dice game - all game data (players, scores) is stored only on your device (local storage) and is never sent to us.

Data collected by third-party services

The App uses two third-party services that collect limited data automatically, as described below.

Google AdMob (advertising)
Used to display banner and interstitial ads in the App. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and other device/usage data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

RevenueCat (in-app purchases)
Used to manage the optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase and restore purchases across devices. RevenueCat collects your purchase history and an anonymous app-specific user identifier. See RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Why we collect this data

  • Advertising ID / device data (AdMob): to display and measure in-app advertising.
  • Purchase history / anonymous ID (RevenueCat): to unlock and restore the "Remove Ads" purchase you paid for.

Data sharing

Data described above is shared only with Google AdMob and RevenueCat, strictly to provide the functionality listed above. We do not sell your data, and we do not otherwise share it with third parties.

Data security

All data transmitted by these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

Your choices

  • You can limit ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • You can purchase "Remove Ads" to stop ads (and AdMob data collection for ads) within the App entirely.
  • You can request deletion of data held by AdMob or RevenueCat by contacting us at the email below, or directly via Google's/RevenueCat's own privacy tools.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
